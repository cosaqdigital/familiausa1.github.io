If you are in the United States and want to follow **Brazil's 2026 presidential runoff**, the key time is **5:00 PM in Brasília on Sunday, October 25**.

That is when the official election calendar says voting closes. After that point, official polling-station data can begin to be issued and processed, and the national count starts updating.

For the easiest experience, use our [Brazil Election 2026 live results page](/brazil-election-2026.html). It automatically displays the poll-closing time in the time zone detected by your browser.

## Official poll-closing time in Brazil

Brazil's Superior Electoral Court, the TSE, states in the official 2026 election calendar that second-round voting runs from **8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Brasília time**.

At 5:00 PM Brasília time, voting closes and polling-station reports begin to be issued.

Official source: [TSE 2026 election calendar](https://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/eleicoes-2026/calendario-eleitoral).

## What time is 5:00 PM Brasília time in the United States?

On **October 25, 2026**, Brasília is on UTC-3.

For the major U.S. time zones on that date, the poll-closing moment corresponds approximately to:

| U.S. time zone | Brazil polls close |
| --- | --- |
| Eastern Time | 4:00 PM EDT |
| Central Time | 3:00 PM CDT |
| Mountain Time | 2:00 PM MDT |

| Hawaii Time | 10:00 AM HST |

## Why is the East Coast only one hour behind Brasília?

Time-zone differences between Brazil and the United States change during the year because U.S. daylight saving time affects many states.

On October 25, 2026, the U.S. is still observing daylight saving time in most places that use it.

That makes Brasília one hour ahead of U.S. Eastern Daylight Time on election day.

This is why 5:00 PM in Brasília corresponds to 4:00 PM in places such as New York, Washington, D.C. and most of Florida.

Some U.S. locations do not follow daylight saving time, so visitors should rely on their local device time rather than a generic chart whenever possible.

## Do results appear exactly when polls close?

Not all at once.

The closing of voting marks the transition from voting to reporting and counting.

Official totals are published progressively as election data is received and processed.

The national percentage counted should rise over time, and candidate totals can change as more polling-station results enter the official count.

That is why our live page refreshes automatically during active counting.

## How quickly does Brazil count votes?

Brazil's centralized electronic voting and national reporting system can produce a rapidly changing national count after polls close.

However, no responsible source should promise an exact minute when the final result will be known.

Technical transmission, processing and the geographic distribution of remaining polling stations can affect how quickly the count becomes decisive.

The best way to follow the election is to watch three things at the same time:

- each candidate's vote total;
- each candidate's percentage;
- the percentage of the count already processed.

## What if I live in Florida?

Most of Florida uses Eastern Time, while part of the Florida Panhandle uses Central Time.

That means a single statewide statement such as “the results start at 4:00 PM in Florida” would be incomplete.

Our live page avoids this problem by calculating the equivalent time from the visitor's browser time zone.

If you are in Eastern Time, the scheduled poll close corresponds to **4:00 PM EDT**.

If you are in the Central Time portion of Florida, it corresponds to **3:00 PM CDT**.
